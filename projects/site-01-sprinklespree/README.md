# Site 1: SprinkleSpree (Kids & Party Confectionery)

**Brand Archetype:** High-Energy, Playful, Colorful Youth & Family Bakery  
**Tagline:** *Baking Happiness in Every Bite!*  
**Core Route Mapping:**
- **Home:** Home 01 (Tri-donut 3D perspective flip hero + floating sprinkles)
- **About:** About 05 (Celebration crew + rotating plus-badges)
- **Contact:** Contact 05 (Party catering & wholesale request desk)
- **Subscribe:** Subscribe 03 (Donut of the Month family celebration box)
- **Why:** Why 05 (1,000,000+ sprinkles stat counter & flavor quiz)

## How to Run:
This brand is rendered through the Donutland multi-tenant engine at route:
`http://localhost:3000/brand/site-01`
or via `NEXT_PUBLIC_BRAND_ID=site-01 pnpm --filter web dev`.
